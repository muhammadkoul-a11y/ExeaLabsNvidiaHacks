04 · Dataset Sheet A-04

What the robot would train on

Each run is written in the LeRobot v2.1 layout that NVIDIA GR00T reads, with an eight-number state and action per frame plus the ego and wrist cameras. Six steps produce it.

  1. Step 1

    Scan the room

    An iPhone LiDAR scan (Apple RoomPlan) captures the walls, counters, sink, stove and fridge. The twin on this page comes from the authored sample. One real kitchen, scanned with the capture app on an iPhone 13 Pro Max, has run through the pipeline; it has no tape measurements yet.

  2. Step 2

    Compile the twin

    The compiler normalises the scan into fixtures with sizes, positions and colours, plus the drawers, doors and hinges they carry. Objects come from a small catalogue with mass and friction.

  3. Step 3

    Run the physics

    MuJoCo steps the room at 500 Hz. People are kinematic capsule walkers on seeded waypoint loops, and every step records their closest approach to the robot.

  4. Step 4

    Record an expert

    A scripted inverse-kinematics expert performs each task. A run counts only if the physics says it worked, such as the drawer being open or the bowl being in the sink.

  5. Step 5

    Export

    The export writes frames, states, actions and two 128 px cameras as LeRobot v2.1 in the GR00T layout.

  6. Step 6

    Validate

    A loader check reads the dataset back the way a trainer would, windows the actions, decodes the videos and reports any problems.

05 · Evaluate Sheet A-05

Results from a small batch

The evaluation harness ran a small batch with the scripted expert while people walked through the room. The figures below come from the export.

Limits of these numbers

    Not done

      Nemotron on Nebius Token Factory plans each task

      • Nemotron 3 Super turns an instruction and the room's inventory into a task plan, live, in about 2.6 s. Nemotron 3.5 Lightning writes the training and held-out wordings.
      • On 15 indirect requests such as "let me check if we're out of milk", the planner picked the right task 13 times. A keyword parser got 6.
      • On 54 wordings Lightning wrote, it was 52 against 47. The requests were written by us, not sampled from users.

      Tavily looks up each scanned appliance

      • Every appliance found in the scan is researched live, and the findings go into the planner's prompt.
      • For the fridge it returns the federal limit on the force needed to open an empty door, 66.7 N (15 lb), citing the eCFR and GE.
      • The safety rules shown beside each answer are our own table, and are labelled as such.

      Revision schedule

      What is built, and what is left

      GR00T N1.7 has been fine-tuned on this dataset: 39 of 48 against 26 of 48 for the clean-data baseline, on small samples, with no base-GR00T or generic-RoboCasa cell. One real kitchen has been scanned and run through the pipeline, but it has no tape measurements, and the room on this page is authored. The hosted demo is not deployed. The pitch is the rendered 24-second video, not a studio recording; it is not on YouTube yet. Below is every open task, grouped by what it needs.

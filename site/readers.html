Read one line of the Arrival film

The film has 37 lines and we want as many voices as we can get. Take one, two or three (three at most), record each on your phone, and send them in. Your face goes on a card in the film, with your name on it, while your line plays: large on the left over the big shots, small in the corner over the code.

How to record your line

  1. Hold your phone in landscape at eye level, in a quiet room, with a window or a lamp in front of you, not behind.
  2. Look into the lens. Wait one second, read your line once, naturally, then wait one more second. Record it three times.
  3. Fit the time under "say it in". The film is fast, so say it with energy, like a trailer. If you run over, speed up rather than cutting words.
  4. Numbers are written the way to say them ("sixty-one", "thirteen of fifteen"). Say "GR00T" as "groot" and "N1.7" as "N one point seven".
  5. Send your best take of each line, named NN_firstname.mp4 (NN is the two-digit line number), to the person who shared this page, with your name as you want it shown.
